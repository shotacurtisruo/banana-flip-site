Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Banana Flip is a single-player game. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. It shows advertising, which is the one thing that involves anybody else, and this policy says exactly what that means.

What the app stores on your device

Everything the game remembers is kept on your device only, using Apple's standard app-preferences storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Coins, and which banana skin and trail you own and have equipped
  • Your best streak, high score, total throws, accuracy and time played
  • Daily-goal progress and your day streak
  • Halloween event candy and quest progress
  • Whether you have purchased Remove Ads

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, which also means progress does not transfer between devices.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), a Unity Technologies service. An interstitial appears roughly every twenty throws, and there are optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. If you buy Remove Ads, the interstitials stop.

Ads are non-personalised. Specifically:

  • The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and never shows

the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it does not track you.

  • Your activity is not linked to your identity, and **not combined with

data from other apps or websites** to target advertising at you.

  • Nothing is sold to data brokers.

To show an ad at all, the ad provider receives a non-personal device identifier — used for things like not showing you the same ad repeatedly and detecting fraud — and the fact that an ad was requested, shown or closed. That is the extent of it. Unity's handling of that data is covered by Unity's Privacy Policy.

What the app still does not do

  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not request your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • It has no account system and never asks for an email address.
  • It does not know your name.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. The app is told only whether a purchase succeeded — it never sees your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Restoring purchases asks Apple what you already own. No personal data reaches the developer.

Saving clips

If you choose to save a replay clip, the app writes that video to your photo library. This happens only when you tap Save, and the app only ever adds — it cannot read or delete anything already in your library.

Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. It is rated 4+ and contains nothing unsuitable, but because it shows advertising it is not designed as a children's app.

Changes

This policy is updated before any version ships that changes what the app collects. A future version is likely to add analytics — anonymous counts of sessions, throws and time played, used to understand how the game is played — and this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will say so before it does.
