Get in touch

Questions, bugs, or anything that went wrong — email shotacurtisruo@gmail.com. It reaches the developer directly. Please include your device and iOS version; it makes most problems much faster to find.

Common questions

My progress disappeared

Everything is saved on your device only — there are no accounts and no cloud save, so progress does not move between devices and is removed if you delete the app. Restoring purchases brings back anything you paid for.

I bought Remove Ads and still see ads

Tap Restore Purchases in the shop. If that does not work, email the address above and say which Apple ID made the purchase — not the password, just the email.

Refunds

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The Halloween event

The Haunting runs every October. Candy, the haunted room and the quests that unlock its skins are available while it is on, and come back next year.
